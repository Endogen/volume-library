Small app, big comfort

Everything you reach for, nothing you don’t

VeraCrypt keeps doing the encryption. Volume Library just makes living with it delightful.

Unlock from the menu bar

Your favorite volumes live one click away. Type the password, and they open in Finder.

Make new containers

Press ⌘N, pick a size and a password. You get a standard AES volume, ready to unlock.

Careful by default

New volumes mount read-only. Passwords go to VeraCrypt over standard input and are never saved.

Eject with confidence

One button per volume, or Eject All when you’re done. Moved files are easy to locate again.

New volume

A fresh encrypted container in seconds

No wizard with twelve pages. Just the three things that matter, then pick where to save it.

  • Standard VeraCrypt format with AES and FAT — opens anywhere VeraCrypt does.
  • Never overwrites an existing file at the destination.
  • Lands in your library automatically, ready to unlock.
Get started

Up and running in three steps

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later.

Install the engine

Get FUSE-T and the matching FUSE-T build of VeraCrypt 1.26.31. Put VeraCrypt in Applications.

Add Volume Library

Download the ZIP, unzip it and drag Volume Library.app into Applications.

Click the lock

Open the app and find it in your menu bar. Choose Add Volume… or New Volume… and you’re set.

This release is signed with an Apple Development certificate and isn’t notarized yet, so Gatekeeper may block the first launch. Apple explains how to open an app you trust.

To verify the download, keep the ZIP and SHA256SUMS.txt from the release page together and run shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt.
